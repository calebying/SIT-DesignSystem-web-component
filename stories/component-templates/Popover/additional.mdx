## Rich content

Unlike `sit-tooltip`'s text-only `content` prop, `sit-popover`'s `content` slot accepts arbitrary
HTML — forms, buttons, other components.

## Placements

`placement` accepts any Floating UI placement (`top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`, and their
`-start`/`-end` variants). The panel automatically flips/shifts to stay in the viewport.
