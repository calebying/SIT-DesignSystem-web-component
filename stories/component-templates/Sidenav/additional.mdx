## Active state 

Both SitSidenavItem and SitSidenavLink have `active` state. 

As a menu type, SitSidenavItem will be automatically assigned `active` to true when one of its SitSidenavLink children is active. When this happens, SitSidenavItem menu will be open on first load

As a link type, add `active` prop to SitSidenavItem to achieve the active styles
